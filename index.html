About Me

I’m Joan Maquinano, a data analyst transitioning into data engineering. I build reliable pipelines, analytics-ready datasets, and clear dashboards that help people make better decisions. My portfolio combines SQL, Databricks, dimensional modeling, data quality, and business-focused analysis.

In my professional work at Institutional Shareholder Services (ISS), I validate and quality-check sustainability data for more than 5,000 companies. That experience has strengthened my attention to detail, structured QA workflows, and ability to work with high-stakes reporting data.

I hold a BS in Biology, Major in Zoology, from the University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB). My research background shaped how I approach complex questions: understand the context, validate the evidence, and communicate the result clearly. I’m currently focused on growing as a data engineer through collaborative GitHub projects and practical pipeline work.
